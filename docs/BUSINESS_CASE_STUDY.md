## PART 1: COMPETITOR CASE STUDIES

### 1.1 FIVETRAN — The Gold Standard

**Business Model:**
- **Revenue:** $500M+ ARR (2024)
- **Valuation:** $5.6B (2021), likely $8-10B now
- **Customers:** 6,000+ companies
- **Pricing:** Usage-based (per row synced)
- **Average Contract Value (ACV):** $80,000/year

**Pricing Tiers:**
| Plan | Price | Rows/Month | Connectors |
|------|-------|-----------|------------|
| Free | $0 | 500K | 10 |
| Starter | $500/mo | 5M | 50 |
| Standard | $2,000/mo | 50M | 200 |
| Enterprise | $5,000-50,000/mo | Unlimited | 700+ |

**How They Make Money:**
1. **Usage-based pricing** — customers pay per row synced
2. **Enterprise contracts** — $100K-1M+ annual deals
3. **Data volume scaling** — costs grow with customer data
4. **Premium connectors** — some connectors cost extra
5. **Professional services** — implementation, training

**Key Metrics:**
- **Gross Margin:** 75-80%
- **Net Revenue Retention:** 150%+ (customers spend more over time)
- **CAC Payback:** 18-24 months
- **LTV/CAC:** 5-8x

**What We Can Learn:**
- Usage-based pricing scales with customer success
- Enterprise contracts are 80% of revenue
- Net retention >100% means customers stay and grow
- Premium support is a profit center
