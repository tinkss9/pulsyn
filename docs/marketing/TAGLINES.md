# Pulsyn Taglines

Taglines organized by channel and use case. Pick the right tone for the right surface.

---

## Website Hero (Primary)

1. **"Real-time data. Zero drama."** — Clean, confident, positions against complex CDC tools.
2. **"320 connectors. One platform. Sub-second latency."** — Concrete numbers, instant credibility.
3. **"CDC without the Kafka tax."** — Direct pain-point hit for teams evaluating Debezium + Kafka stacks.

## Social Media (Twitter/X, LinkedIn)

4. **"Your database changes. Your warehouse knows instantly."** — Descriptive, shareable, no jargon.
5. **"We certified 320 connectors so you don't have to."** — Highlights the certification work as a moat.
6. **"Stop building ETL pipelines. Start replicating."** — Challenges the status quo, sparks engagement.
7. **"CDC for teams that have production to worry about."** — Speaks to ops-minded engineers.

## Paid Ads (Google, Reddit, Dev.to)

8. **"Postgres to Snowflake in 3 clicks. Try free."** — Specific use case + low friction CTA.
9. **"The CDC platform your AI agents can talk to."** — MCP angle, unique differentiator.
10. **"Replace your Debezium + Kafka stack. Save 80%."** — Aggressive comparison, cost angle.

## Email Subject Lines

11. **"Your data pipeline is lying to you."** — Curiosity gap, opens well in cold outreach.
12. **"320 connectors certified. Is yours on the list?"** — Personalized, drives click-through.
13. **"CDC doesn't have to hurt."** — Empathy-first, good for nurture sequences.

## Developer Community (Hacker News, Dev.to, Reddit)

14. **"We open-sourced the CDC engine. The UI is the business."** — Transparency builds trust with devs.
15. **"MCP-native CDC: let your AI agents manage replication."** — Technical differentiator for AI-forward teams.
16. **"Apache 2.0 core. No vendor lock-in. Run it yourself."** — OSS credibility, anti-lock-in positioning.

## Enterprise / Outbound Sales

17. **"The last data pipeline you'll ever build."** — Bold, aspirational, good for exec decks.
18. **"Sub-second replication. 99.9% SLA. SOC 2 coming."** — Enterprise-grade signals.
19. **"From 320 certified connectors to your custom ones in days."** — Highlights speed of connector development.

## Brand Tagline (All Surfaces)

20. **"Data flows. You ship."** — Short, memorable, works on billboards and loading screens alike.

---

## Usage Guidelines

- **Website hero:** Use #1, #2, or #3. Rotate quarterly.
- **Social posts:** Use #4-7. Vary by platform — LinkedIn gets #7, Twitter gets #5.
- **Google Ads:** Use #8 or #10 as headline, #9 as description.
- **Email campaigns:** Use #11-13 as subject lines. A/B test each.
- **Dev community:** Use #14-16 in posts, comments, and launch announcements.
- **Sales decks:** Use #17-19 in slide headers.
- **Brand consistency:** #20 is the evergreen brand line. Use on footer, loading states, swag.
