**Research date:** 2026-08-06
**Purpose:** Identify highest-value SaaS connectors for Pulsyn CDC platform — which platforms serve the most businesses and would generate the most connector demand.

## Methodology Notes

- **Primary sources:** DemandSage (statistical aggregation), BuiltWith (website technology detection), W3Techs (web technology surveys), company earnings reports/IR pages, Statista
- **Market share figures** are based on revenue share unless otherwise noted (e.g., website technology share is based on BuiltWith crawling)
- **"Confidence" ratings:** High = sourced from company filings or 2+ independent statistical sources; Medium = single source or industry estimate; Low = anecdotal/unverified
- **Web search was unavailable** during this research session; data was gathered via direct URL fetching from known statistical sources. Some categories (Analytics, Project Management) relied more heavily on well-established industry knowledge cross-referenced with available sources.
- All dollar figures in USD unless otherwise noted
