# Task: Get 10 Beta Users with PostgreSQL + Snowflake

## Objective
Acquire 10 beta users who have the PostgreSQL → Snowflake use case for Pulsyn.

## Target Customer Profile
- **Company:** Startups or mid-size companies
- **Use Case:** Real-time CDC from PostgreSQL to Snowflake
- **Budget:** $500-2,000/month
- **Pain Point:** Current tools (Fivetran, Airbyte) are too expensive or too slow
- **Technical Level:** Data engineers or backend developers

## Acquisition Strategy

### 1. Direct Outreach (Week 1-2)
- **Target:** Companies using PostgreSQL + Snowflake (find on LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub)
- **Message:** "We're building real-time CDC for PostgreSQL → Snowflake. Sub-second latency, $499/mo flat. Want to beta test?"
- **Channel:** LinkedIn DM, Twitter DM, email
- **Goal:** 5 beta users

### 2. Community Engagement (Week 2-3)
- **Target:** Data engineering communities
- **Channels:**
  - Reddit: r/dataengineering, r/snowflake, r/postgresql
  - Hacker News: Show HN post
  - Discord: dbt, Snowflake, PostgreSQL communities
  - Slack: Data engineering groups
- **Message:** "We built a CDC tool that's 10x cheaper than Fivetran. Looking for beta testers."
- **Goal:** 3 beta users

### 3. Content Marketing (Week 3-4)
- **Content:**
  - Blog post: "Why We Built Pulsyn" (technical deep dive)
  - Comparison: "Pulsyn vs Fivetran: Real-time CDC at 1/10th the Cost"
  - Tutorial: "Setting Up PostgreSQL → Snowflake CDC in 5 Minutes"
- **Channels:** Dev.to, Medium, company blog
- **Goal:** 2 beta users

### 4. Product Hunt Launch (Week 4)
- **Preparation:**
  - Create Product Hunt page
  - Prepare demo video
  - Get 10 upvotes from friends
- **Launch:** "Pulsyn — Real-time CDC without Kafka"
- **Goal:** 5+ signups, 2 beta users

## Beta User Benefits
- **Free Pro access** for 3 months
- **Direct Slack channel** with founding team
- **Priority support** — we'll fix bugs for you
- **Influence roadmap** — tell us what connectors to build next
- **Early access** to competition

## Beta User Requirements
- Must have PostgreSQL database (production or staging)
- Must have Snowflake account (any tier)
- Must be willing to provide feedback (1 call + weekly survey)
- Must allow us to use their logo (with permission)

## Success Metrics
- 10 beta users signed up
- 5 beta users actively replicating data
- 3 beta users providing feedback
- 1 beta user willing to be case study

## Timeline
- Week 1-2: Direct outreach (5 users)
- Week 2-3: Community engagement (3 users)
- Week 3-4: Content marketing (2 users)
- Week 4: Product Hunt launch (bonus)
